ROM provenance
==============

Both ROM images are byte-for-byte copies from the 32-bit KolibriOS CHIP-8
emulator by rgimad (GNU GPL v2), see:

  kolibrios/programs/emulator/chip8/roms/
  (upstream ROM collection: https://github.com/dmatlack/chip8/tree/master/roms)

ibm.ch8      132 bytes  sha256 8bf3b46d8a64c2074e7538200f684a2eaced258404d3c7d3bd7a917c3d0143e5
             Draws the IBM logo, then spins on a jump-to-self at 0x228.
             Used for the deterministic 256-instruction boot leg.

tetris1.ch8  494 bytes  sha256 667cb026dee03f59f3a2fd81a2ffeab47da87731883f9601d37ba019976f94dd
             TETRIS game, keys: 4 = rotate, 5 = left, 6 = right.
             Loaded after Enter switches from the boot leg to interactive mode.

The sha256 digests are mirrored as assembly-time constants in ../api.inc and
asserted by tests/stage2-app/chip8_model.py.
